##  What is JalSetu?

JalSetu is an AI-powered disaster-response coordination platform that acts as a bridge between people affected by disasters and people who want to help.

### Our workflow

** Need →  Prioritize → Match →  Coordinate → Track**

---

##  Why JalSetu?

Many disaster platforms provide alerts, maps, news and information.

JalSetu focuses on something different:

> **What happens next?**

We connect an actual need with available resources and help prioritize and coordinate the response.

##  AI Layer

JalSetu's intelligence layer is designed around:

1. **Priority Assessment**
2. **Resource Matching**
3. **Relief Insights**
4. **Future Demand Prediction**
5. **Resource Optimization**

The current version is a hackathon prototype using demo data.
